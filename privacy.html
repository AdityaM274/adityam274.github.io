Privacy policy

This policy describes PulseCast Desktop Publisher, an owner-operated Windows application maintained by Aditya Mittal for four owned publishing brands. It has no external user registration.

Information accessed

  • Google and YouTube: OAuth access and refresh tokens; the selected channel's identifier and identity information returned by the API; video identifiers, titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, scheduled publishing times, and upload, processing and publication status.
  • Local video inputs: reviewed video files and their publishing metadata, used to make the requested uploads.
  • Facebook and Instagram: separately authorized account or Page identifiers, access tokens, media metadata, upload/session identifiers and publishing status.

The application does not request YouTube analytics for its current publishing workflow. Authorization permissions are used for the publishing functions described here, not unrelated account access.

Purpose and transfers

Information is used to identify the correct owned destination, upload selected media, request schedules, check provider responses, resume interrupted work and prevent duplicate operations. Video files and publishing metadata are sent to the platform selected for that upload. Credentials are sent only as needed to the relevant platform's authorization and API endpoints.

The publisher does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use Google API information to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Our use and transfer of Google API information follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Credentials are stored in local credential files. The local publishing database and progress records can contain account and video identifiers, publishing metadata, upload-session information and error classifications. They are retained on the operator's PC until the operator removes them. Credentials and upload-session URLs are not included in these public information pages.

The operator controls local files and access permissions. This is not a claim that any storage method eliminates all security risks. Records copied or backed up by the operator remain subject to the operator's control.

Revoke access or remove local data

The operator can disable the publisher and remove its access through Google Account third-party connections. Google provides instructions for reviewing and removing access. Meta access can be reviewed or revoked through the relevant Facebook or Instagram account settings.

Revoking authorization does not itself erase previously stored local files or remove videos already uploaded to a platform. To remove local data, first stop the publisher, then delete the applicable local credential files and publishing records, including any backups. Removing the publishing database loses its local resume and duplicate-prevention history. Manage or delete already uploaded media through the relevant platform.

Website and provider information

These information pages contain no registration form or OAuth sign-in form. Hosting-provider privacy information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google and Meta handle information received by their services under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.
