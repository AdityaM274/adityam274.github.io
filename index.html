PulseCast Desktop Publisher

A local publishing tool for videos from four owned brands: GEO, LPA, OVB and FOC.

Operated by Aditya Mittal, PulseCast prepares and tracks authorized video uploads and publishing schedules for the operator's YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts.

This website provides application and privacy information. It does not offer public user registration or access to other people's accounts.

What the desktop application does

  • Uses Google OAuth to connect the selected owned YouTube channel and confirm its identity.
  • Uploads reviewed videos and their titles and descriptions, requests publishing schedules, and reads upload, processing and publication status.
  • Uses separately authorized Meta APIs for the operator's Facebook and Instagram accounts.
  • Keeps credentials, a publishing queue and progress records on the operator's Windows PC so work can be inspected and resumed.

Access and control

Google and Meta authorization occurs through their own account authorization flows. The website does not request passwords or receive OAuth tokens. Publishing depends on valid authorization, ready local inputs and platform acceptance; a local queue entry is not proof that a video is live.
